You are an elite startup strategist, senior product designer, and full-stack AI architect.

Your task is to design a modern SaaS platform called “KHDA Compass” — an AI-powered education intelligence platform focused on schools in Dubai regulated by the KHDA (Knowledge and Human Development Authority).

## CORE GOAL

Build a platform that becomes:
“The definitive intelligence layer for Dubai schools.”

This is NOT just a school directory.

* Zillow for schools
* Glassdoor for education
* AI advisor for parents
* Dubai-focused education OS

1. Parents in Dubai
2. Expat families relocating to UAE
3. Students planning university pathways
4. Education consultants
5. Schools seeking analytics

1. AI School Matchmaking

* Match schools based on:

  * budget
  * curriculum
  * area/location
  * commute
  * child personality
  * learning style
  * university goals
  * extracurricular interests

2. KHDA Report Analyzer

* Parse KHDA inspection reports
* Generate simple summaries
* Highlight strengths/weaknesses
* Compare schools year-over-year
* Use AI to explain reports in plain English

3. Smart School Comparison

* Compare any 2–5 schools side-by-side
* Show:

  * fees
  * KHDA rating history
  * academics
  * facilities
  * teacher turnover
  * parent satisfaction
  * commute estimates
  * university placements

4. Fee Transparency System

* Calculate total yearly cost:

  * tuition
  * uniforms
  * books
  * transportation
  * hidden fees
  * annual increases

5. Anonymous Parent Reviews

* Structured reviews
* Anti-spam moderation
* Ratings for:

  * bullying
  * academics
  * administration
  * communication
  * homework pressure
  * inclusivity
  * extracurriculars
